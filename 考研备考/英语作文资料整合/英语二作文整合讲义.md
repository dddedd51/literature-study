## 02 小作文：一项任务对应一组信息

### 先填六个格，再写第一句

E01第3页把审题归纳为背景、体裁与内容、语域与格式。执行时写成六格：**谁写 → 给谁 → 为何写 → 必须写什么 → 用什么格式 → 字数与署名**。题干要求两件事，就在草稿上画两个勾选框，写完各自再打勾。

| 交际功能 | 必写内容 | 可用的基础句（本次整理） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 建议／推荐 | 明确建议＋与收件人需求有关的理由 | I suggest that you choose ... because ... |
| 道歉 | 道歉事项＋必要解释＋可执行补救 | I am sorry that ... To make up for this, I will ... |
| 邀请 | 活动＋时间地点＋对方参与方式 | I am writing to invite you to ... |
| 感谢 | 对方的具体帮助＋实际效果 | Thank you for ... Your advice helped me ... |
| 申请 | 申请岗位＋两条相关能力或经历＋可联系性 | I would like to apply for ... I have experience in ... |
| 求助 | 当前问题＋希望获得的具体帮助 | Could you please help me ...? |
| 投诉 | 事实问题＋影响＋合理诉求 | I am writing about a problem with ... I would appreciate ... |
| 建议＋鼓励 | 可执行建议＋理由＋支持与鼓励 | You could ... This may help you ... I am sure you can make progress. |

三段可作为组织方式：开头说明目的；中间落实题干任务；结尾感谢、期待或再次致歉。熟人邮件不必机械介绍自己的学校身份；申请信中的背景需要与岗位有关。**题干已给事实不能改写成相反事实；未给的合理细节只能服务写作情境，不能假称来自原题。**

格式选择与语域保持一致。信件／邮件有称呼、正文、结束语和署名；通知有标题、必要日期、事项与发布者；会议纪要围绕会议事实。不是所有体裁都写“Dear Sir or Madam”。来源：[E01 · PDF第3页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%EF%BD%9C27%E5%B0%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E6%8A%80%E5%B7%A7%E7%B2%BE%E8%AE%B2%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0%E3%80%90%E5%85%AC%E4%BC%97%E5%8F%B7%EF%BC%9A%E8%80%83%E7%A0%94%E6%96%AF%E5%9F%BA%E3%80%91.md#page-3)、[E01 · PDF第6页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%EF%BD%9C27%E5%B0%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E6%8A%80%E5%B7%A7%E7%B2%BE%E8%AE%B2%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0%E3%80%90%E5%85%AC%E4%BC%97%E5%8F%B7%EF%BC%9A%E8%80%83%E7%A0%94%E6%96%AF%E5%9F%BA%E3%80%91.md#page-6)、[E02 · PDF第10页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-10)、[E02 · PDF第35页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-35)。

### 完整示范：道歉＋替代方案

**新编练习情境：** 你答应周六接朋友James，但临时需要参加重要考试。写邮件道歉并提出替代安排，署名Li Ming，约100词。它借鉴E02第26页“无法接机”的任务类型，考试原因和安排为本次练习设定。

> Dear James,
>
> I am sorry that I cannot meet you at the airport this Saturday as planned. I have to take an important exam that morning, so I will not be able to arrive on time.
>
> To help you reach the hotel, I suggest taking the airport bus. It is easy to find and stops near your hotel. I will send you the route and a map before you leave. If you have any difficulty, please call me. I can meet you after the exam and help you settle in.
>
> Please accept my apologies. I look forward to seeing you soon.
>
> Best wishes,
> Li Ming

检查：第一段明确道歉对象与原因；第二段提供交通、信息和后续帮助；结尾简洁。不能删掉补救办法，只留下感谢与抒情。来源练习：[E02 · PDF第26页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-26)；原库还有2023年“艺术展／机器人展”建议邮件可用于换题复写。

## 03 通知与会议纪要：先选对格式

### 通知要让读者知道怎样参加

**新编示范，活动日期、地点与报名方式为练习设定：** 学生会邀请国际学生参加欢迎活动，介绍安排，约100词。

> Notice
>
> November 12, 2026
>
> All international students are invited to a welcome party at the Student Activity Center. The event will begin at 6 p.m. on November 15 and last about two hours.
>
> There will be music, short performances and an introduction to campus clubs. You will also have the chance to talk with Chinese students and try some traditional snacks. The party is free, and no special preparation is needed. Please register at the student office by Friday so that we can prepare enough seats and food.
>
> We look forward to meeting you and helping you feel at home on campus.
>
> The Students' Union

从E02的通知练习提取结构：对象、活动、时间地点、参与方式、发布者。不能把“通知”写成给某一个人的私人感谢信。对照：[E02 · PDF第10页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-10)、[E02 · PDF第11页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-11)。

### 会议纪要记录已讨论的事实

先看题干要求的会议时间、地点、参会人、主题、主要讨论、结论、后续行动；记录会议经过通常用过去时，后续计划按时间关系表达。不要照抄通知里的“欢迎参加”，也不要编造原题已经指定的会议决定。

**新编结构示范（短提纲，非完整交卷范文）：**

> Minutes of the Class Meeting on Food Waste
>
> Time and place: 2 p.m., November 12, Classroom 201.
>
> The class discussed how to reduce food waste. Members agreed to order smaller portions and share reminders in the dining hall. Two volunteers were asked to design a poster before Friday. The class monitor would collect feedback at the next meeting.

这里的日期、人物安排均为练习设定。正式写作按具体字数补足讨论要点，避免把提纲当完整约100词答案。E02相应练习位于[E02 · PDF第35页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-35)至[E02 · PDF第38页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-38)。

## 04 大作文：先判断输入形式

| 材料形式 | 第一段做什么 | 主体段做什么 | 避免什么 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 有时间序列的图表 | 交代对象、区间、起终点与主要趋势 | 谨慎解释变化、说明影响 | 编造图上没有的中间年份或统计结论 |
| 同时点分类图表 | 比较最大、最小、主要差距 | 分析选择、结构或特点 | 把类别差异写成时间上的增长 |
| 图画 | 写人物、动作、场景与关键对比 | 解释寓意、给例证或建议 | 不看画面就硬套“经济发展” |
| 图画＋图表／双图 | 两份材料各交代关键信息 | 说明联系，同时保留单位与对象差异 | 漏掉一幅材料；直接把相关性写成因果 |
| 文字材料评论 | 准确概括作者观点或争议 | 明确立场、解释理由、承认适用边界 | 改成无关图表模板；只重复原文 |

E04第12页强调“英文指令＋数字信息＋文字说明”，这三个部分一起读。每次写下：**对象、时间、单位、分母、比较维度、题干动作**。例如“各年级学生兼职比例”的分母是各自年级人数，不是把全校兼职学生分成四份。来源：[E04 · PDF第12页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%EF%BD%9C27%E5%A4%A7%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E6%8A%80%E5%B7%A7%E7%B2%BE%E8%AE%B2%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0%E3%80%90%E5%85%AC%E4%BC%97%E5%8F%B7%EF%BC%9A%E8%80%83%E7%A0%94%E6%96%AF%E5%9F%BA%E3%80%91.md#page-12)、[E04 · PDF第17页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%EF%BD%9C27%E5%A4%A7%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E6%8A%80%E5%B7%A7%E7%B2%BE%E8%AE%B2%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0%E3%80%90%E5%85%AC%E4%BC%97%E5%8F%B7%EF%BC%9A%E8%80%83%E7%A0%94%E6%96%AF%E5%9F%BA%E3%80%91.md#page-17)、[E02 · PDF第61页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-61)、[E02 · PDF第73页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-73)。

### 数字表达练到不出错

| 表达任务 | 稳定写法 | 自检 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 从20%升到30% | The proportion rose from 20% to 30%. | 增加10个百分点，增长率50%，二者不同 |
| 数量增加100 | The number increased by 100. | by为变化量；to为终点 |
| 保持约三成 | The figure remained at around 30%. | 有估读误差时保留around/about |
| 两项分别为40%、25% | The figures for A and B were 40% and 25%, respectively. | 主体顺序与数字顺序一致 |
| A是B的两倍 | The figure for A was twice that for B. | 倍数比较同一单位、同一统计口径 |
| 最大类别占40% | A accounted for 40%, the largest share. | 最大份额不一定过半；不自动写majority |
| 多选调查65%、48%等 | 65% of respondents selected A. | 可多选，总和可超过100%，不能硬凑成饼图 |

这些是本次规范化示范。E04汽车图练习的OCR把“25%”误成“5%”，原图已核，不能照OCR背数字；E02继续学习图明示多项选择，总和可超过100%。对照：[E04 · PDF第16页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%EF%BD%9C27%E5%A4%A7%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E6%8A%80%E5%B7%A7%E7%B2%BE%E8%AE%B2%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0%E3%80%90%E5%85%AC%E4%BC%97%E5%8F%B7%EF%BC%9A%E8%80%83%E7%A0%94%E6%96%AF%E5%9F%BA%E3%80%91.md#page-16)、[E02 · PDF第46页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-46)。

### 完整示范：继续学习的目的

**资料图表信息，已看原图：** 2025年某市在职群体继续学习目的：提升岗位技能65%、学习新领域48%、个人兴趣37%、文凭或证书26%；可多选。下文为本次新写参考，不是原作者范文。

> The bar chart presents the reasons why employed people in a city pursued further study in 2025. Improving skills for their current jobs ranked first at 65%, followed by learning about new fields at 48%. Personal interests and qualifications accounted for 37% and 26%, respectively. Respondents could choose more than one answer, so the percentages do not add up to 100%.
>
> These figures suggest that practical needs played an important role in learning decisions. As workplace tasks change, employees may need to update their skills to remain effective. Meanwhile, interest in new fields suggests a desire to explore different career possibilities. The other two reasons also deserve attention: education can support personal development as well as formal qualifications.
>
> Employers and training providers should therefore offer flexible courses that meet different needs. Learners, in turn, should choose clear goals and assess whether a course helps them apply useful knowledge in practice.

## 05 材料评论：观点必须回应作者

按四步：①用一句话概括原文观点；②说清赞成、反对或有条件赞成；③用两个理由或一个具体例子支持；④给出与讨论直接相关的建议。写“我部分同意”之后必须说明同意哪部分、限制在哪里。

E02第73页起讨论奖励式教育，第95页起讨论儿童转向技术社交。这些是资料中的练习，不将书内“大纲模拟样题”标签视为已独立核实的官方真题。读原题：[E02 · PDF第73页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-73)、[E02 · PDF第95页](%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8BMarkdown/27%E7%9F%B3%E9%9B%B7%E9%B9%8F%E4%BD%9C%E6%96%87%E5%86%B2%E5%88%BA%E8%83%8C%E8%AF%B520%E7%AF%87%28%E8%8B%B1%E4%BA%8C.md#page-95)。

**新编训练情境：** 作者认为物质奖励能短期改善儿童行为，但长期可能削弱内在动力。回应这一观点。下文为本次示范：

> The passage argues that rewards may improve children's behavior in the short term but weaken their motivation over time. I largely agree, although small rewards can be useful when they support a clear learning goal.
>
> If a child reads only to receive money, reading may become a task to finish rather than an activity to enjoy. Once the reward disappears, the habit may disappear too. However, encouragement does not have to take the form of payment. Parents can discuss an interesting story with the child or recognize the effort involved in solving a difficult problem. Such responses help children notice the value of the activity itself.
>
> Parents should therefore use material rewards carefully and gradually encourage independent choices. Clear expectations, helpful feedback and time spent together can support lasting habits. The goal is not simply to obtain immediate obedience, but to help children understand why an action matters and become willing to repeat it.

不要为了套“社会应该营造氛围”忽略真正能行动的主体——这里首先是父母及儿童的具体互动。
